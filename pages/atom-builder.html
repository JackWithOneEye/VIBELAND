      HOW TO USE

      PROTONS decide which element you have. NEUTRONS make different isotopes of it and decide whether the nucleus is stable. ELECTRONS decide the charge: fewer electrons than protons makes a positive ion, more makes a negative ion.

      Use the buttons, type a number, or press P N E to add a particle (hold Shift to remove one). MAKE STABLE picks the nearest stable isotope, DECAY! lets a radioactive nucleus decay one step, and DECAY TO STABILITY follows the whole decay chain.

      Stability and half-lives come from real data for every stable nucleus and many famous radioactive ones. For everything else, the page estimates with the liquid-drop model of the nucleus, so treat masses and decay modes marked "estimate" as good guesses.
