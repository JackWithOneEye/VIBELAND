      The Rules (Hong Kong style, simplified):
      • Everyone holds 13 tiles. On your turn you draw one and discard one by clicking it.
      • Win (Mahjong!) with 14 tiles: four sets and a pair, or seven pairs. A set is a pung (three alike), a kong (four alike, you draw an extra tile) or a chow (three in a row of one suit).
      • Anyone may claim a discard for a pung, a kong or Mahjong; only the next player may claim it for a chow. Mahjong beats pung, pung beats chow.
      • Fan: Mahjong 1, self-drawn 1, concealed hand 1, each dragon pung 1, pung of your seat wind or of East 1, all pungs 3, seven pairs 3, half flush 3, full flush 6, all honours 8.
      • A hand is worth 8 points at 1 fan, doubling with every fan up to 256. Self-drawn: all three others pay. On a discard: the discarder pays double.
      • Four hands, the dealer (East) moves on each hand. Everyone starts with 500 points – the highest score wins.
