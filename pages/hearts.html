How to play

  • Pass 3 cards: left, right, across, then keep. Click cards to pick them.
  • The 2♣ leads the first trick. Follow suit if you can.
  • No ♥ or Q♠ on the first trick (unless that's all you have).
  • Hearts can't be led until one has been played.
  • Each ♥ costs 1 point, the Q♠ costs 13.
  • Take all 26 points to shoot the moon: everyone else gets 26.
