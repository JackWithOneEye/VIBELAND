🕹️ How To Use

  • Type a function of x in a box, the graph updates as you type
  • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to zoom in
  • Hover to trace a curve; it snaps to roots, peaks, valleys and crossings
  • Use t in a function and press ▶ Play to animate it
  • Click a colour square to hide or show its graph

🧮 What You Can Type

  • + - * / ^, brackets, |x|, x!, 2x, 3sin(x)
  • sin cos tan sec csc cot and arcsin…, sinh…, arctanh…
  • e^x exp ln log log2 sqrt cbrt abs
  • floor ceil round sign gamma min max mod
  • Constants pi π e tau phi; sin^2(x), x^(1/3) work too
