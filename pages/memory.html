❓ How To Play

  • All cards lie face down – every picture appears exactly twice
  • On your turn, flip two cards. A matching pair is yours and you go again
  • No match? The cards turn back over and the next player takes a turn
  • When all pairs are found, the player with the most pairs wins
  • Solo: find all pairs in as few moves as possible
